### Do the new warnings affect goal achievement?

No. The goal clause is "the RND-01 asset gate is amended to admit exactly that one inline icon". Evidence the clause holds: the gate requires exactly one `<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="data:image/svg+xml,...">` of a fixed attribute order, rejects every other icon form and data: URI, and now also closes the two bypasses the previous review raised (character references; non-allowlisted elements such as SMIL). The two new gaps are further ways a deliberately or accidentally malformed future edit could slip a variant payload past the validator. They do not change what ships, and they do not make the gate admit the current icon or any other icon today without a contrived edit that is itself unlikely (a raw newline inside a token, or a `filter` attribute). Because the gate's job is to guard future edits, I rate these Warnings, not Blockers, and treat them as test-hardening debt rather than goal failure. This is the same class and severity the initial verification recorded for the previous round of the same finding family, so the verdict does not change.

row up, a score that counts up instead of jumping, a flipper press that skips the tally in the live
game, a tick per row, and the drain sound back on the drain step (PLSH-06 presentation, D-12, D-14).

prefers-reduced-motion once for every later presentation plan (06-09 shake reuses it).

wiring, and the observer's drain-counter and bonusTick pushes.
</objective>

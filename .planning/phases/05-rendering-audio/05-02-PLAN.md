<objective>
Put the real table on screen: replace the throwaway debug renderer with a procedural
space-themed TableRenderer that draws at the display's true resolution and stays smooth on
high-refresh displays.

Purpose: RND-01 (everything drawn in code, zero image assets) and RND-02 (stable 60 fps via
render interpolation). It fixes the two concrete render gaps research found: the backing store
is about 17x oversized, and flipper angles are not interpolated.

Output: view-transform, palette, static-layer bake, TableRenderer, flipper snapshots in the
loop, interpolation helpers, the canvas recorder, the asset gate, and the live wiring with a
`?debug=1` escape hatch to the old renderer.

<objective>
Make the plunger honest before nudge exists: a release launches only a ball that is
really resting on the plunger, and edges that land in the same 8.3 ms window are applied
in the order they happened, so a quick re-press or a very fast tap is never lost or stuck.

Purpose: these are the Phase-3 review findings IN-01 and IN-02, which STATE.md asks to
triage before Phase 4 builds on the plunger. They matter more now: the 3-ball loop (GAME-02)
serves every ball to the plunger, and a nudge (GAME-03) can shove a ball that sits in the
plunger lane. The plan touches only the plunger control and input modules, so it runs in
wave 1 beside the 04-01 tracer.

Output: the rest-speed gate, PlungerInput.isHeld(), PlungerController.applyEdges(), and
unit plus real-table proofs. main.ts switches to applyEdges in 04-05, the plan that owns
main.ts's step callback in wave 3.
</objective>

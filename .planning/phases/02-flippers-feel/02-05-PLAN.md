## Phase Goal

**As a** pinball player, **I want to** control two flippers with the keyboard that snap up instantly, fling the ball realistically, and hold a steady cradle, **so that** the moment-to-moment pinball feel is satisfying and faithful to Space Cadet.

> This plan turns the story's "fling realistically", "steady cradle", and "can never be passed through" clauses into seeded, replayable proofs -- the phase's headline artifacts, mirroring Phase 1's tunnel-proof gate.

<objective>
Prove the two Core-Value properties instead of demonstrating them (02-CONTEXT `<specifics>`): (1) the FLIP-04 rotational anti-tunnel guarantee, by extending the Phase-1 swept-containment soak (D-03) into a flipper-primary non-penetration gate driven at feel-tuned peak omega across a dense seeded phase-offset sweep — GREEN with sub-stepping ON, and a teeth-bearing negative control that goes RED with sub-stepping forced OFF at the same peak omega; and (2) the feel properties — tip-vs-base momentum transfer (`v = omega * r`, FLIP-02) and a jitter-free "heavy, not floaty" cradle (FLIP-03 / D-09) — as deterministic numeric tests.

Purpose: this is the phase's proof discipline. "I flung it and it held" is rejected (D-03 carries forward Phase-1 D-03/D-04). The negative control toggling SUB-STEPPING (not CCD) is what proves the D-01 mechanism specifically holds the property, and the feel proofs settle the "heavy" verdict Phase 1 explicitly deferred here — as regression-guarded numbers, not a vibe.
Output: a seeded green FLIP-04 gate (npm run soak), a fast bounded green + teeth negative control (npm test), and tip-vs-base + cradle feel proofs.
</objective>

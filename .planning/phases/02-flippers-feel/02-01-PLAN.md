## Phase Goal

**As a** pinball player, **I want to** control two flippers with the keyboard that snap up instantly, fling the ball realistically, and hold a steady cradle, **so that** the moment-to-moment pinball feel is satisfying and faithful to Space Cadet.

> Note: the ROADMAP Phase-2 `**Goal:**` line is written as an outcome statement, not a strict `As a / I want / so that` user story. The story above is a faithful restatement of that goal + its four success criteria (the player is the user). This plan delivers the physical flipper geometry that the story's "fling realistically" and "steady cradle" clauses stand on.

<objective>
Productize the Phase-1 single stress test flipper into TWO real, mirrored, feel-shaped capsule flippers on the throwaway test rig (D-07/D-11), add the `tipRadius` field the capsule needs to the engine-agnostic `FlipperState` contract (D-08), re-map the motor drive hook to feel speeds (D-05 adapter side / D-04), kill caught-ball micro-bounce with `restitution = 0` (D-06(3)), and teach the throwaway debug renderer to draw a stadium so the new shape is visible.

Purpose: this is the physical foundation of the Core Value. The rounded tip removes the sharp-corner snag so the `v = ω × r` tip-vs-base difference reads cleanly (FLIP-02) and the ball catches/rolls cleanly for a jitter-free cradle (FLIP-03). The fat convex tip is also the fastest, most tunnel-prone point — a good CCD target for the sub-stepping guarantee that lands in plan 02-04.
Output: two mirrored capsule flippers returned by `getFlippers()` with distinct ids and `tipRadius`, drivable at feel speeds through the existing `setFlipperMotor` hook, drawn as stadiums by the debug renderer.
</objective>

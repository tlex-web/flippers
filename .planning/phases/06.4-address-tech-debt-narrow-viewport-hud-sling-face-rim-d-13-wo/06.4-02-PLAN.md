and geometry, physics, spec and kick behavior do not change.

<decisions>
There is no CONTEXT.md. The rim spec is the planner's choice from 06.4-RESEARCH.md "Sling Rim (item 2)". The user judges it by eye at the 06.4-04 UAT:
- Color `SLING_RIM = "#7d82a8"` (SLING_BODY's slate hue, lightened). Width `SLING_RIM_LINE_M = 0.015` m (half of RUBBER_LINE_M), which is about 1.4 to 2.0 CSS px across 375 to 1440 viewports.
- Edges: every segment index not equal to `el.kickFace` (segments 0 and 1 on today's slings). Do not re-derive the face geometrically: 06.3 D-09 removed the longest-segment rule, and resolve() validates `kickFace`.
- Order: body fill, then rim, then rubber (Pitfall 7). The rubber's round caps then cover the rim at the two kick-face corners.
- No meaning color: amber (SLING_RUBBER) means "kicks" and cyan (WALL) means "guide wall" (palette header: "One meaning per color").
- Contrast numbers measured at plan time (WCAG 2.x relative luminance): SLING_RIM against BG_TOP 5.38, against BG_BOTTOM 5.35, against SLING_BODY 3.62. SLING_RUBBER against BG_BOTTOM is 11.43, and SLING_BODY against BG_BOTTOM is 1.48 (today's edge). Against the composited playfield the research measured 4.61.
- If the UAT asks for a lighter or darker rim, any value at 3:1 or more passes the same tests. Tuning happens only after UAT feedback, in a follow-up, never inside this plan.
- Out of scope: 06.3 IN-03 (the obtuse corner kicks within about 0.049 m on the passive face). The rim makes that face visible, but the kick zone does not change.
- Base for the render-only proof: 41275c6, the HEAD when this phase was planned (planning-only commits follow it).
</decisions>

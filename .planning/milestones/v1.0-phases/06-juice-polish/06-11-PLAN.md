<objective>
Persist and celebrate the best score: a guarded localStorage-backed HighScore read once at boot and
written only on a new best at game over, shown as HIGH SCORE on attract and game over, with a NEW
HIGH SCORE announcement, a pulse and a fanfare (PLSH-08, D-15, D-16).

Purpose: the high score is the only I/O in the phase and its key is a costly decision (D-15); it
lives behind one injected, node-testable seam so every failure mode is guarded in one place.

Output: high-score.ts, the session submit and getters, the storage injection, the HUD best line and
status row, the markup and pulse, and the highScore push.
</objective>

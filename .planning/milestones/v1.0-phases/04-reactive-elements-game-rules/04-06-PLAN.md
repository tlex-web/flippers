<objective>
Let the player nudge the table, and punish over-nudging with a real accumulate-and-decay
TILT that kills the ball's scoring until it drains.

Purpose: GAME-03 and roadmap SC#5, plus the tilt-on-the-last-ball end of GAME-02. Nudge
reuses the generic applyImpulse hook (velocity only, so no teleport and no tunneling);
tilt is a pure step-counted meter, so it replays deterministically. The HUD lines that
show the ball number, the prompts, WARNING and TILT arrive in 04-07 (split out of this
plan to keep it inside its context budget). The flipper drop on tilt arrives in 04-08,
which adds FlipperController.setEnabled.

Output: nudge keys, TiltMeter, the tilted session state, and spy-world, unit and
real-composition proofs.
</objective>

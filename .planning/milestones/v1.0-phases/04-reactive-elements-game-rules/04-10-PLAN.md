<objective>
Close G-04-6 (cosmetic, UAT test 6): make every piece of on-screen text readable at the
fitted display size.

Two independent defects, both measured in the UAT run at 1280x684, dpr 1.5:
1. The FPS / Physics Hz / Kinetic E readout is painted at 14 px in canvas logical space
   (debug-draw.ts:494). The canvas (1512x2708 logical) is CSS-fit to 367x657, about 0.24x,
   so the text lands at about 3.4 px. **Chosen fix: move the readout to the DOM.** That is
   the same move 04-01 made for the score HUD ("DOM text, crisp at any canvas fit"). Scaling
   the canvas font by 1/fit instead would make the readout's box about 800 logical px wide
   and cover half the table.
2. The controls hint (index.html:82) uses "/" both as the separator and as the Slash key
   name, and its 220 px max-width wraps mid-group ("left / flipper"). **Fix:** a five-row
   key/action list with kbd key caps, the "3-row key/action list" fix that 04-UI-REVIEW #6
   and 04-REVIEW IN-05 recommend. It is 12 px, per the UI review's typography floor.

Purpose: portfolio-grade legibility of the HUD (GAME-01's on-screen HUD). This also clears
the STATE.md Phase 3 -> 5 carry-forward "the HUD ignores the display fit and renders at
about 3.4 px".
Output: DiagView in src/ui/hud.ts, the #hud-diag panel, the renderer without canvas text,
and the rewritten hint.
</objective>

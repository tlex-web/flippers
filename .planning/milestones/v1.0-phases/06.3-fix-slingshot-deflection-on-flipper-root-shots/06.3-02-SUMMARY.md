## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `ownTouched` is set on the first sling contact only, not on any own-sling contact**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (RED run)
- **Issue:** The plan contradicts itself. Its `flip()` step sets `ownTouched` on any own-sling contact while armed. Its decisions block says the gate classifies by the first sling contact, "matches the research probe, so the printed numbers compare 1:1". Built literally, the RED read 75 own (52 down) and 16 opposite (2 down), against research's 61 (49) and 30 (2). A diagnostic proved the rig was identical: first-contact classification gave 61 (49) / 30 exactly, and 14 flips touched the opposite sling first and then their own. At t = 0.28 the literal reading would fail GREEN on 1 flip: a cross shot that ricochets off the opposite sling into its own sling and is knocked down. Under first-contact classification that flip is one of the 2 knocked down inside the cap.
- **Fix:** The recorder copies only the first sling contact, and sets `ownTouched` when that first contact is the own sling. The FlipResult doc states the rule.
- **Files modified:** src/harness/root-shot.test.ts
- **Verification:** RED 61 (49) / 30, and the bracket and GREEN lines match research exactly.
- **Committed in:** 3e8b086 (the RED commit)

### D-13 wording drift (explicit report)

- **Goal text:** "reach the upper field without *first* touching their own bat's sling". This is a first-contact criterion, and the harness implements exactly that (`root-shot.test.ts` recorder: `if (first !== null) return; ... if (e.elementId === own) ownTouched = true`). Under user ruling A ("approved A") the goal as written is met: 0/94 gated flips first-touch their own sling.
- **CONTEXT.md D-13 text** still reads "with no contact with its **own** bat's sling before that" (any own contact before clearing). That is stricter than what is implemented. The harness header/`FlipResult` doc comment does state first-contact, but the `describe` title ("clears its own sling") and the `it` title overstate it.
- **Measured size of the gap** (verifier's own scratch run tracking ANY own-sling contact before clearing): exactly one in-band flip differs, bat 1 inlane k=94, along 0.44. It first hits sling 300 at (0.87, 4.60) (opposite), then its own sling 301, and does not clear. It is one of the 2 allowed knocked-down cross shots. The review's other two such flips (k=102, k=96) are at along 0.465 / 0.462, outside the gated band.
- **Judgment:** not a gap. The roadmap goal explicitly allows up to 2 knocked-down cross shots, ruling A is the user's, and 06.3-REVIEW-DISPOSITION records CR-01 as skipped with a doc follow-up. The follow-up (amend D-13 text, root-shot header/test titles) is still outstanding, recorded as an advisory above. A future reader of CONTEXT.md alone would believe a stricter gate than the code enforces.

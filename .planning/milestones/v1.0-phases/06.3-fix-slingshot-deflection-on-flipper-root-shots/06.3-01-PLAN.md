<objective>
Name each slingshot's kick face explicitly (D-09), before 06.3-02 trims the slings.

Today `deriveKickFace` takes the longest segment of the sling triangle. Plan 06.3-02 slides the
lower-right corner up the hypotenuse to t = 0.28 (D-14). That shrinks the hypotenuse to 0.266 m, under
the 0.75 m vertical inlane edge. The longest rule would then kick the ball into the inlane and draw the
rubber and sparks on the inlane wall. This plan carries a `kickFace` segment index from TABLE_SPEC
through `resolve()` into `deriveKickFace`. It deletes the longest rule and makes `resolve()` fail loudly
on a missing or bad index.

Purpose: the kick, the rubber stroke, the flash and the sparks stay on the hypotenuse whatever its
length (D-08, D-10). A guard proves it on a trimmed fixture, RED first.

Output: the `kickFace` field, the `SLING_KICK_FACE` constant, the resolver field and its validation, the
rewritten `deriveKickFace`, the D-09 guard and the resolver cases. The live table is unchanged: at
today's corner the named face is the face the longest rule already picked.

Spec-less probe fallback: skipped. The phase has no requirement IDs to probe (visible skip).
</objective>

### CR-01: Bar hint flickers on bounce apexes during every weak-plunge fall-back (violates D-01 "only while the ball rests")

**Files modified:** `src/game/tuning.ts`, `src/game/game-session.ts`
**Commit:** 3b6e4e1 (RED contract 449a2b8)
**Applied fix:**
- `PLUNGER_HINT_SETTLE_STEPS = 12` in a new "Bar hint (06.4.1.1)" section of tuning.ts: 100 ms at 120 Hz, 4x the longest CR-01 apex window (3 steps).
- A private `restSteps` count in `GameSession`, written by one added statement in `tick()` (`activeRestingInZone() ? restSteps + 1 : 0`), and reset to 0 on serve (`serveBall()`), re-serve (`reServe()`) and drain (`service()`).
- `isBallOnPlunger()` returns `restSteps >= PLUNGER_HINT_SETTLE_STEPS && activeRestingInZone()`. The live term drops the signal on the read right after a release. Nothing in the sim reads the count, so step behavior and both replays are unchanged. game-session.ts and tuning.ts are add-only against 9a26577.
- Teeth: M1 (`PLUNGER_HINT_SETTLE_STEPS = 1`) turns the served-ball e2e, the WR-01 holds 10 and 40 and the rig apex-window case red; M2 (live term dropped) turns the rig live-term case red; M3 (re-serve reset removed) turns the rig re-serve case red. The serve and drain resets are defense in depth (no ball is in play for at least a tick on those paths).

# Phase 06.4.1.1 Plan 05: Settled plunger hint signal Summary

**`isBallOnPlunger()` now reports ready only after 12 consecutive resting fixed steps (a private `restSteps` count in `tick()`), so the "Hold Space to launch" bar hint no longer blinks at weak-plunge bounce apexes. The sim and both determinism replays are untouched.**

## Accomplishments
- CR-01 closed. The hint signal waits `PLUNGER_HINT_SETTLE_STEPS` (12 steps, 100 ms) of continuous rest. Bounce apexes rest for at most 3 steps, so they never read true. On the real table, holds 10 and 40 now turn ready once, at true rest (speed exactly 0), and stay ready for 60 steps.
- WR-01 tightened. The fall-back e2e now asserts sustained readiness and exact-0 speed. Before, it stopped at the first apex.
- A serve shows the hint on the 12th fixed step counting the Enter step, for every ball. A backstop re-serve restarts the count.
- IN-01 fixed. The false-before-start case now uses a resting spy ball that carries id -1, so it fails if the `NO_BALL` guard is removed.
- `game-session.ts` and `tuning.ts` are add-only against 9a26577. The protected paths and manifests are byte-identical, and both replays are green.

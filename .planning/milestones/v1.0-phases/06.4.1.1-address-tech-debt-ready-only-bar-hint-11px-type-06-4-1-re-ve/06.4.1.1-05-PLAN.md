Claude's discretion, chosen here:
- Name and value: `PLUNGER_HINT_SETTLE_STEPS = 12` (the review's name; 12 steps = 100 ms at 120 Hz, 4x the longest
  apex window in the CR-01 trace).
- Add-only shape. The existing `resting` statement in `tick()` stays byte-identical and the counter is a separate,
  added statement, even though `activeRestingInZone()` then runs twice on an idle step. It is pure and allocation-free
  (a loop over the pooled ball array), and add-only keeps `git diff -U0 9a26577 -- src/game/game-session.ts` free of
  removed lines: the plainest proof that no existing step line moved. The getter, added in 06.4.1.1-01, is absent from
  9a26577, so rewriting its body and JSDoc still diffs as added lines.
- The getter keeps the live rest term: `this.restSteps >= PLUNGER_HINT_SETTLE_STEPS && this.activeRestingInZone()`.
  The count is taken from the snapshot at the top of the step; the live term drops the signal on the read right
  after the release step instead of one step later.
- Resets: `serveBall()`, `reServe()` and the drain path of `service()` (directly after `this.active = NO_BALL;`). Only
  the re-serve reset is observable on the spy rig. On the serve and drain paths no ball is in play for at least one
  tick, which already zeroes the count, so those two resets are defense in depth per D-10, recorded in the SUMMARY and
  not mutation-tested.
- WR-01 runs for holds 10 and WEAK_HOLD_STEPS (40): the two rows of the review's trace with a fall-back and more than
  one apex. Hold 0 is left out because `launch(g, 0)` presses and releases inside one input drain.

  read ready at release+294 and release+468, with speed exactly 0, and stay ready for the rest of a 1200-step window;

  `PlanckWorld.addBall` pushes the new ball's snapshot into the pool at once (planck-world.ts:641), so the Enter

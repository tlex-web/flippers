## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **The sling's lower face is nearly invisible, and root shots hit it** (WARNING, `needs_human_review: true`). This is the user-reported problem in `.planning/todos/pending/2026-11-23-root-shot-deflects-off-slingshot.md`, which owns the geometry fix and stays out of scope here. The visual half: `drawSlingshot` (src/render/static-layer.ts L283-295) strokes only the kick face in SLING_RUBBER. The lower face, (0.86, 4.6) to (1.16, 4.75), is the unstroked SLING_BODY `#2b2d42` edge against a navy playfield, about 1.3:1. The ball bounces off a surface the player cannot see as solid, so the deflection feels arbitrary. **Fix:** when the todo reshapes that face, also give the non-kicking sling edges a thin rim, for example a lighter SLING_BODY tint at about half of RUBBER_LINE_M. Then whatever surface remains reads as a hard edge.
2. **The HUD/Controls panel covers the playfield at narrow viewports** (WARNING, pre-existing; open since 04-UI-REVIEW and predicted by 05-UI-REVIEW). At 768x1024 the panel (top-left, about 268x305 px) covers the left skill lane, its chevron and the top of the left lane guide. At 375x812 it covers the whole top third: all three skill lanes and both upper bumpers. index.html has no `@media` rule. **Fix:** below about 900 px wide, collapse the Controls list behind a toggle (keep the SCORE and status lines), or move the panel under the table.
3. **The tilted ball-search kick plays with no cue** (WARNING, introduced by 06.2-02 by design). `getBallSearchCount()` (src/game/game-session.ts L861) is read only by tests. Nothing in presentation consumes it. While tilted, a ball resting in a hinge pocket jumps sideways after 2 s with no sound or flash, and the player can take that for a physics glitch. **Fix:** in the app loop, emit a short coil thunk through the cue queue when the count increments, optionally with a FLASH_CONTACT pulse on the ball, the way a real machine's ball search fires audible coils.

Minor recommendations:
- 06-UI-SPEC.md L206 still places the inlane arrows at (0.585, 4.72) and (2.315, 4.72). The code derives them from the sensor (lamps.ts `ARROW_RISE_M`) and they now sit at (0.4645, 4.37) and (2.4355, 4.37). Reword the row as "ARROW_RISE_M 0.23 up-table of the inlane sensor midpoint" so the next audit does not raise a false mismatch.
- `/favicon.ico` returns 404 on every load, the only console error on a clean boot. Pre-existing portfolio polish: add a procedural SVG data-URI icon in index.html.

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (4/4)
- 06.2 added or changed no user-facing string. `git diff b5757a8..HEAD -- index.html src/ui` touches only key-labels.ts (L65-77: a doc comment, plus the merge of two guards into `typeof raw !== "string" || !PRINTABLE.test(raw)`).
- Live check at 1440x900 (`desktop.png`): every Controls cap is non-blank and single-glyph where it should be. The layout map resolved Y and `-` for the flipper keys, and the layout-only note shows. No blank or garbled cap appears, so the WR-04 merge kept the 06.1 behaviour.
- Ball search adds no copy, and none is needed. The HUD status already reads `TILT`, then `TILT · NO BONUS` on the forfeit (hud.ts L165-168).

### Pillar 2: Visuals (3/4)
- **WR-01 verified in pixels.** At 1100x2200 (about 284 px/m), the left lane line runs from x ≈ 40 to 253 and the right from x ≈ 592 to 806. Both start inside the funnel wall's glow. A row scan at 2200x4400 (`junction-left.png`, `junction-right.png`) shows the funnel core ending at canvas px 54 with the lane starting at 58 on the left, and the lane ending at 1150 with the core starting at 1154 on the right. The 0.0165 m sensor inset is symmetric and reads as touching at normal scale. The old 0.215 m float is gone.
- **Arrows centred.** The arrow x positions (147 and 698) match the lane midpoints (146.5 and 699) to the pixel, and they sit near the middle of the open column between the outer wall and the sling. At the old x = 0.585 the left arrow sat about 44 px right of the midpoint.
- **Glint on the midpoint.** In `glint-left.png` and `glint-right.png` the INLANE_LIT star appears at the lane midpoint directly under its arrow, and the FLASH_SENSOR lane flash spans the whole new lane from wall to sling.
- **Collider matches art.** In `inlanes-zoom-debug.png` the dashed sensor segments in `?debug=1` overlay the baked lane lines exactly. This closes 06.1-UI-REVIEW fix #1 ("no visual evidence of the moved slings/arrows").
- 06.1-UI-REVIEW fix #2 (sling top vertex inside OPEN_FIELD_REGION, possibly crowding the bumpers) is visually resolved. At 1440x900 the sling tops sit about 0.3 m below and to the side of the bottom bumper.
- **Deduction:** the sling lower-face legibility issue (Top fix 1). It is pre-existing, but it sits in the exact region this phase changed and is the surface behind the user's complaint.

### Pillar 3: Color (4/4)
- The unlit arrow is the dark teal INLANE_UNLIT `#0e3b37`. The lit arrow, including steady-lit after a pass (`after-inlane-pass-live.png`, both arrows lit), is INLANE_LIT `#2ec4b6` with its halo. The inlane glint and motes use INLANE_LIT, matching 06-UI-SPEC L113-114 and L133.
- The lane insert stays LANE at LANE_ALPHA 0.55 (static-layer.ts L440-446). The sensor flash uses FLASH_SENSOR `#d9ff66` (flash.ts L51), as in phase 05.
- No palette file changed in 06.2.

### Pillar 4: Typography (4/4)
- No type change in 06.2.
- index.html declares 4 font sizes (11, 12, 16 and 32 px) and 2 weights (400 and 700), both through `var(--mono)`. That is within the 4-size / 2-weight bar.
- Caps render as `<kbd>` text through textContent (T-04-36 holds).

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)
- The arrow centre sits ARROW_RISE_M 0.23 above the sensor, which leaves about 0.12 m of clear space between the arrow stem and the lane line. The 0.0165 m lane-to-wall inset sits under the wall glow, so there is no visible notch at 1x.
- The right lane ends 0.02 m short of the sling vertex (x 0.84 against 0.86) and the left mirrors it. The ends meet cleanly in every crop.
- **Deduction:** the pre-existing HUD overlap at 768 and 375 px (Top fix 2). Confirmed in pixels for the first time: the canvas is 550x984 at x = 109 on tablet and 361x646 at x = 7 on mobile, and the fixed panel sits on both. There is no horizontal scroll (scrollWidth equals innerWidth at both sizes).

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)
- Inlane pass feedback works end to end in a live game, captured on the exact frame: lane flash, glint at the sensor midpoint, motes, then the arrow lit steadily for the rest of the ball. The clear-on-serve rule is covered by tests, not captured.
- Ball search is soft-lock-safe by construction. It runs only while tilted, takes at most 3 x 240 steps (6 s at 120 Hz) before the forfeit, and its state resets on serve, re-serve and tilt (06.2-02). The forfeit reuses the existing drain path, so the HUD shows the normal TILT and bonus flow. **Deduction:** the kick has no presentation cue (Top fix 3).
- Root-shot sling deflection: open, with severity major in its todo. It is not deducted here because 06.2 did not cause it (sling geometry unchanged since fb4e81d; lanes 500 and 501 are sensors). It remains the largest open experience defect on the table, and it hits the core value (flipper feel and shot control).
